# Ancre de personnages — édition 2

Références : `docs/art/Reference_pictures/cadets.png`, `Frankly.png`, `letitia.png`,
`zacharie.png`. Elles fixent les signes distinctifs, sans imposer leur rendu photographique.

Production avec le générateur intégré ; ancre inspectée et validée : six cadets jeunes,
aplats nets, identités distinctes, Franklyn/Letitia/John puis Abigail/Zachary/Grover.
Fichier : `art-masters/comics/holt-12-pages/v2/references/CAST-ANCHOR.png`.

Prompt :

> Create a production character model sheet for an original French cyberpunk comic,
> inspired by the highly stylized 2D animation energy of Cyberpunk Edgerunners. Transform
> realistic HOLT game identity references into genuinely drawn anime/comic designs,
> not painted realism. Landscape 3:2, six waist-up designs, two rows of three, ivory
> background; simple expressive angular faces, large readable eyes and strong eyebrows,
> clean black ink silhouettes, flat saturated cel colors, one hard polygon shadow per
> skin region. No pores, photoreal hair strands, gradients, realistic cloth, or 3D.
> Upper row FRANKLYN / LETITIA / JOHN; lower ABIGAIL / ZACHARY / GROVER, labelled.
> Franklyn: light tan, short spiky black hair, symbolic freckles, stern, navy zip collar,
> cyan shoulder stripes. Letitia: light brown skin, honey-gold graphic curls, loose high
> bun, lively smile, navy lapels, black tie, silver shoulder bars. John: very short silver
> hair, pale, square jaw, reserved. Abigail: tan, two black braids, fierce. Zachary: brown
> skin, black bowl haircut, friendly grin. Grover: pale tan, black longer diagonal fringe,
> slim, ironic. Navy simplified uniforms, small HOLT shoulder emblem. Redraw fully.
