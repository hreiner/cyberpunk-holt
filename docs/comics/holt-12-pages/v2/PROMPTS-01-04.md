# HOLT v2 — prompts des pages 01 à 04

## Cadre commun de génération

Chaque bloc « Page » ci-dessous est **un seul prompt de planche entière**, en portrait 2:3. Il décrit toutes ses cases et leur ordre de lecture. Fournir à chaque appel `v2/references/CAST-ANCHOR.png`, la page témoin validée par le directeur artistique, puis une ou deux références **originales** adaptées à la scène après inspection ; une page v2 précédente peut compléter le raccord, cinq images de référence au maximum. Ne jamais employer les PNG de la première édition. L'ancre fixe les identités, les références de jeu donnent seulement l'architecture, les accessoires et la situation ; le dessin doit rester une nouvelle interprétation 2D.

Invariants pour chaque page : energetic original French cyberpunk animation comic, not a screenshot; bold angular ink contours, clean flat saturated colors, hard cel-shadow shapes, expressive graphic faces, sharply simplified navy cadet uniforms, cinematic foreshortening, varied gutters, motion crossing panel borders. Night-blue/cyan/magenta with acidic yellow accents; amber at the ball, dirty red at the attack. No photo texture, no painterly skin, no 3D rendering, no copied *Edgerunners* character or costume. Maintain stable casting from the anchor: Franklyn = teen boy, short pointed dark hair, strong eyebrows, a few freckles; Letitia = teen girl, medium-brown skin, voluminous honey curls, lapelled navy uniform and tie; John = teen boy, very short silver hair, square reserved face and **no implant**; Abigail = teen girl, tan skin and long braids; Zachary = teen boy, brown skin and black bowl cut; Grover = lean teen boy, long dark hair and slanted fringe. Their faces, hair and uniform silhouettes must stay distinct.

Lettering is part of the **generated art**: draw each exact French line listed for its panel once only, in a spacious cream oval or organic ink speech balloon with a thin black outline and a slender curved tail reaching the named speaker's mouth. A shout uses a jagged balloon; sound effects are hand-drawn lettering embedded in the action. No rectangular dialogue cards, no floating text, no English words, no accidental duplicate speech, no speech balloon over eyes or hands. `HOLT` may appear only as environmental signage where requested. Keep gutters and reading order unmistakable, left to right and top to bottom. Put small page number in lower exterior margin (`01`, `02`, `03`, `04`). Do not add an illustrator's signature.

These prompts adapt the delivered chronology and branch in `../STORY.md`, whose source list points to `docs/design/03-CHAPTER-1.md` (last school day, teams, practical exercise and dance) and `docs/chapters/ch2/GAME-DESIGN.md` (ball attack and Franklyn's shouted warning). `v2/BRIEF.md` fixes the visual and lettering rules. No additional plot beat is implied by the dramatic framing.

## Page 01 — Choisir (cinq cases)

**Page geometry.** Five clearly separated panels, portrait 2:3: one wide shallow sunrise establishing panel across the top, two energetic small panels across the middle, then two progressively larger bottom panels. The bottom right panel carries the final team choice. Avoid uniform strips. Motion/eye-lines pull the reader down toward Letitia. Use cold academy cyan with a first small shaft of warm dawn light. No weapons or combat yet.

**Full-page prompt.** Draw a complete original French cyberpunk animated comic page with exactly five panels, in the following reading order and with the shared style/casting rules above.

1. **Wide top panel, high oblique exterior/interior cutaway.** HOLT's hard geometric academy blocks at dawn beyond the barracks window; Franklyn springs from the lower bunk into his simplified navy cadet jacket, still seventeen and slightly sleepy. A small environmental academy plaque reads `HOLT`; a hand-written `S` has been added after four institutional letters on a notebook corner, a quiet solidarity motif, not another person. Thin cyan window light; a sense of the last ordinary morning. One small organic thought balloon from Franklyn, exact text: **« Dernier jour. »**
2. **Middle left, extreme close-up action insert.** Franklyn's pen scratches the final line of an exam paper; his dark, freckled face reflected in a slanted desk screen; other cadets are simple graphic silhouettes behind. Diagonal speed strokes from wrist to paper. No readable exam questions or extra lettering. **No dialogue.**
3. **Middle right, low angle in the courtyard.** An instructor's arm lifts two team-color markers, blue and red; Franklyn and Abigail step toward opposite sides of the frame, eye contact and competing smiles. Other cadets blur behind. One small balloon from instructor, tail unmistakably to the instructor: **« Deux capitaines. À vous. »**
4. **Bottom left, medium shot with motion.** Franklyn pivots and extends an open hand toward John; John, silver crop and no implant, meets it with a short decisive fist touch. Blue marker streaks behind them. Franklyn's balloon: **« John, avec moi. »**
5. **Bottom right, larger triangular group composition.** Franklyn turns and points toward Letitia, who steps into the blue side with an alert, amused face; on the far red side Abigail hooks an arm toward Zachary and Grover, clearly forming the other trio. All six are visible once; no cloned cadet. Franklyn's balloon above his shoulder: **« Letitia aussi. »** Abigail's smaller balloon on the opposite side: **« Zachary. Grover. Avec moi. »** The two tails must reach Franklyn and Abigail respectively. End the panel on Letitia looking back at Franklyn, not on a victory pose.

**Exact letter audit:** `Dernier jour.` / `Deux capitaines. À vous.` / `John, avec moi.` / `Letitia aussi.` / `Zachary. Grover. Avec moi.` Nothing else besides the HOLT plaque and page number. The small academy `S` is a drawn motif, not an explanatory text block.

## Page 02 — L'épreuve (six cases)

**Page geometry.** Six panels with staggered sizes: top wide vehicle establishing panel; two small opposing tactical panels; a large diagonal central taser impact panel spanning the page; two shorter bottom aftermath panels. The impact panel must be the visual hinge, with its movement crossing one gutter. Clear left-to-right/top-to-bottom sequence. Dusty Badlands ochre against blue/cyan training lights. The weapons are visibly training tasers; blue electric darts, **no bullets, no blood, no dead cadet**. Neither team's overall victory is declared.

**Full-page prompt.** Draw one complete original French cyberpunk animated comic page, exactly six panels, consistent with page 01 and the shared casting rules.

1. **Top wide travelling shot.** A HOLT training van brakes before the container examination yard in the Badlands; a blue team marker flickers inside. Through the front/side windows, only Franklyn, John and Letitia are clearly seen, bracing together as it stops. Slanted horizon and dust arc give momentum. John says in a small oval balloon: **« On entre ensemble. »** Tail to John's silver-haired mouth.
2. **Upper middle left, overhead insert.** Franklyn's hand points across a simplified training-yard sketch and then toward a real gap between stacked containers in the background; Letitia ducks into the line of cover. No extra writing on the sketch. Franklyn's compact balloon: **« Je couvre l'angle. »**
3. **Upper middle right, low-angle red-side counter-move.** Abigail swings around a container, long braids trailing; Zachary crouches beside her and Grover slips forward behind. Their red training strips are visible, no hostile villains. Abigail's balloon: **« On les contourne ! »**
4. **Dominant central diagonal action panel.** Two blue taser darts streak across intersecting paths from the opposing team positions; show one crackling against a padded training vest, safely neutralizing a cadet who remains alert, while Letitia slides behind waist-high cover and Franklyn pulls his arm away from the line of fire. John advances in the depth of frame. Strong foreshortening, sharp cyan sparks and a drawn, legible **« TZAK ! »** sound effect at the vest. No firearm muzzle flash or lethal wound. **No other text.**
5. **Bottom left, kinetic medium shot.** John vaults a low training barrier; Letitia rises from cover to tag a red target with her training taser, making the team coordination visually explicit. Her curls and lapelled jacket remain legible despite motion. Letitia's balloon: **« Maintenant, John ! »**
6. **Bottom right, wider release.** Instructor raises a stop hand between the regrouping blue and red trios. A cadet earlier tagged by a taser sits upright rubbing their padded vest, irritated but safe; Franklyn and Abigail exchange a breathless competitive grin. The scene reads as a completed **non-lethal** practical examination. Instructor's balloon: **« Fin de l'exercice ! »** Tail to instructor, not to cadets.

**Exact letter audit:** `On entre ensemble.` / `Je couvre l'angle.` / `On les contourne !` / `TZAK !` / `Maintenant, John !` / `Fin de l'exercice !` and page number only. Avoid scoreboards or declared winner.

## Page 03 — Encore un tour (cinq cases)

**Page geometry.** Five panels: one lively wide group-photo panel at top; two small mid-row panels for the developing invitation; one expansive dance panel occupying most of lower middle; one thin, irregular bottom panel that keeps the dancers in motion while the closed doors enter the frame. Use more amber and warm magenta than prior pages. The photo is happy and unthreatened; the end suggests a change of rhythm without previewing the attackers. Keep all six identities distinct and countable in the photograph.

**Full-page prompt.** Draw one complete original French cyberpunk animated comic page, exactly five panels, sharing stable faces with page 01/02.

1. **Top wide frontal photograph-in-the-making.** Six teen cadets crash together for a timer photo after the practical exam, laughing rather than posing stiffly. From left to right arrange Franklyn, Letitia, John, Abigail, Zachary, Grover (or a comparably clear grouping), each person once. Zachary leans in with a ridiculous two-finger gesture behind Abigail; Abigail tries to suppress a laugh. A tiny camera flash blooms at the image edge, not a seventh person. Zachary's balloon: **« Gardez celle-là ! »** Its tail reaches his bowl-cut face.
2. **Mid-row left, quick motion insert.** Freshly printed photo flips from the camera into Franklyn's hand; the six faces remain visibly six graphic miniatures, no tragic foreshadowing. One drawn small **« CLIC »** near the camera; no speech.
3. **Mid-row right, close two-shot at the ball.** Under warm lamps, Franklyn extends a slightly uncertain hand; Letitia, honey curls and lapelled navy jacket softened for the party, catches it mid-step with a mischievous smile. Cake and strings of lights are simplified behind them. Franklyn's balloon: **« Tu danses ? »** Letitia's balloon: **« Avec toi ? »** Point the tails to their own mouths, keeping hands clear.
4. **Large lower-middle swooping dance panel.** Franklyn and Letitia turn together, coats and curls tracing one large flowing circular motion that breaks a gutter. Behind them Abigail and Zachary spin in a second smaller circle; John and Grover move at the side of the dance floor, still present but not extra couples invented. Low camera, warm amber floor reflections against cyan ceiling beams, expressive joy in both principal faces. Letitia's airy oval balloon: **« Encore un tour. »**
5. **Thin but legible irregular bottom panel.** Continue the **same** turn from a wider viewpoint: Franklyn and Letitia sweep past the camera foreground, still smiling. Beyond their motion, heavy closed hall doors tremble almost imperceptibly under a narrow red seam of light; the dancers have not noticed. No visible attacker, bullet, wound or explosion yet. **No dialogue or SFX.**

**Exact letter audit:** `Gardez celle-là !` / `CLIC` / `Tu danses ?` / `Avec toi ?` / `Encore un tour.` and page number only. Avoid a readable warning on the doors.

## Page 04 — À terre (quatre cases, impact dominant)

**Page geometry.** Four panels, portrait 2:3. A narrow top suspense panel; a vast jagged main panel filling roughly the central two-thirds of the page for the shattered entrance and Franklyn's shouted warning; two lower consequence panels divided on a harsh diagonal. The breach is the page's main impact, almost a splash, but the four panels and their reading order must remain clear. Amber party light shatters into white flash and dirty red. Kinetic, frightening, **non-gory** violence. Show both Letitia's grave injury and Zachary's first wound while protecting Abigail. All aggressors are anonymous armed figures with a simple scorpion emblem; do not assign identities or invent dialogue for them.

**Full-page prompt.** Draw one complete original French cyberpunk animated comic page, exactly four panels, continuing immediately from page 03.

1. **Narrow top cinematic insert.** Extreme close-up of the closed hall door lock deforming from the other side; a dancer's amber reflection fractures across it. The first crack is marked by one hand-drawn **« KRRK »**. No intruder shown yet.
2. **Huge central impact panel, daring low wide-angle perspective.** Hall doors explode inward in a bright angular white burst; three anonymous armed men stride through smoke, the nearest with a clear black scorpion patch on the sleeve. Splinters and tables cut the composition diagonally; six cadets occupy different depths, not a neat lineup. Franklyn, recognizable short dark hair and freckles, turns away from Letitia to shout **to everyone in the hall** instead of tackling her; his open mouth and outstretched arm are unmistakable. A single large organic **jagged shout balloon**, tail to Franklyn: **« À TERRE ! »** Hand-drawn impact lettering near the shattered door: **« KRAAAM ! »** Preserve visible portions of warm ball decor to make the transition brutal. Do not show Letitia hit in this panel; that action belongs to panel 3.
3. **Bottom left, abrupt close action consequence.** Letitia is struck in the torso as she tries to drop, then collapses against Franklyn's reaching hands. Her face is shocked but conscious; a dark spreading stain on her jacket and shirt shows a **grave injury** without exposed anatomy or gore. John, silver-haired, lunges toward her in the background. Franklyn's small cracked oval balloon: **« Letitia ! »** Tail to Franklyn, not Letitia. Keep her face unobscured.
4. **Bottom right, protective counter-movement.** Zachary throws himself around Abigail to shield her, taking his **first** wound high in the shoulder/back of his dark uniform. His bowl cut and Abigail's long braids are visible; Abigail catches his weight and turns in alarm. One short Abigail balloon: **« Zach ! »** Tail to Abigail. No death posture: Zachary remains conscious, braced on one knee, ready to flee. Red speed lines and one small abstract impact star; no gore.

**Exact letter audit:** `KRRK` / `À TERRE !` / `KRAAAM !` / `Letitia !` / `Zach !` and page number only. One shout addressed to the whole room is the branch-defining choice. No narration explaining the choice, no duplicate cry, no accidental muzzle pointed at the wrong body.
