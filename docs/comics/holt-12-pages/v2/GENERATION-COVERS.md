INTEGRATE ALL COVER LETTERING DIRECTLY INTO THE GENERATED ART, professional graphic comic title. Generous breathing room, no faces covered by text, no rectangular blank panel overlay. No extra speech bubbles on cover. Print-ready clean border, actual completed illustrated cover, no mockup.
