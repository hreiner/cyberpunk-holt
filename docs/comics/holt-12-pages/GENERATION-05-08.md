## Prompt commun transmis

Original angular French cyberpunk animation comic, expressive inked faces, clean hard-edged shadows, restrained cyan/magenta neon, warm amber for the ball, red fire and cold sewer blue as scene-specific accents. 2D illustration, consistent character identities from references, intimate cinematic framing, no existing anime character likeness, no readable text, no lettering, no speech bubbles, no logos, no gore. Reserve a quiet top band (~20%) and bottom strip (~15%) for later lettering; keep faces and hands in the central 65%.

Image 1 CAST-ANCHOR is the ONLY style authority and canonical six-face source. Other references guide identity, place, or previous-panel continuity only; never use their photographic treatment. Dark navy coats. Letitia: medium-brown skin, curly bun, ceremonial navy LAPEL jacket, BLACK TIE, extra SILVER RANK BAR, never Franklyn's zipped armored coat. Franklyn: short dark hair, freckles. John: silver-white crop, no implant. Abigail: twin black braids. Zachary: dark brown skin, dark bowl cut. Grover: tan, long black fringe. Child: dark skin, short dreadlocks, gray hoodie, about ten. No text, letters, logos, bubbles, borders, or gore.
