Outil intégré ImageGen, reconstitution de couverture à partir de la scène de groupe
et des références d'identité/décor. Génération non transparente. Typographie finale
éditable ajoutée ensuite à l'assemblage. Les cinq images jointes et le portrait original de Franklyn ont été réellement ouverts.


1. Page17 composée : distribution, identités Wendy/enfant et ancre comics.
2. Grover original : identité observateur, cheveux noirs mi-longs, cravate.
3. Abigail originale : tresses, traits, uniforme.
4. John original : cheveux platine très courts, proportions sobres.
5. Façade BluePurple originale : bar discret bleu/violet, architecture industrielle.

```text
Use case: illustration-story / identity-preserve.
Asset: premium finished vertical 2:3 comic-book COVER illustration, 1024×1536.
Recompose the ensemble scene in reference1 into a striking original cover for the
existing French cyberpunk comic HOLT — BLUE PURPLE, chapter3. References2–4 fix
original facial identities and uniforms; reference5 fixes the BluePurple facade.
Reference1 fixes the angular Edgerunners-inspired anime-comics rendering, existing
Wendy and child identities, and saturated cyan/magenta night palette. Match that
inked visual language closely: precise expressive linework, strong black shapes,
cel shadows, elegant selective halftone, dramatic restrained rimlight. Not realism.

A rainy Night City street outside the modest BluePurple bar, industrial overhead
walkways and towering night architecture, deep cyan shadow and purple neon, wet
reflections and a suggestion of steam at pavement level. A single integrated scene
with a bold diagonal of light and very strong cover composition, not a collage.
Five young adults plus ONE child, exactly six people, all on the same believable
plane. Grover at foregroundleft, attentive east-Asian young man with straight
medium-length black hair and navy NCPD shirt with black tie, one naturally placed
protective hand on the child's upper shoulder. Child next to him with short dark
bowl haircut, oversized dark hoodie. Wendy foregroundright: purple jacket, short
electric blue-and-magenta swept hair, gold hoop earrings, alert defiant expression.
Abigail between and slightly behind them, long black tight twin braids and dark navy
HOLT cadet jacket, watchful focused expression. John behindright, cropped platinum
hair, pale complexion and navy cadet uniform. Franklyn behindleft, short dark hair,
navy cadet jacket with cyan shoulder markings, worried but composed. Grover, Wendy
and Abigail share the visual initiative; Franklyn has equal or smaller scale and
does not dominate. Clearly separate all faces and arms; realistic young slim builds,
normal hand sizes, each person has two arms; avoid complicated intertwined poses.

Make the composition feel like the next volume of the supplied comic, with depth,
graphic silhouettes, atmospheric rain and ominous city scale, not a generic team
promotional photo. Reserve upper20percent as rich dark architecture/sky suitable
for large clean editorial title added later, keep faces below that zone. Leave a
small dark quieter strip at bottom for a chapter line. No page borders or white
paper margins. No dialogue balloons, no captions, no barcodes, no watermark, no
extra characters, no weapons in their hands, no visible violence, no fantasy scenery.
Do not include giant cropped letters from portrait reference screenshots. Draw
only the discreet BluePurple bar neon in the environment if text is needed there;
the large title will be added separately. Preserve every character's identity.
```
